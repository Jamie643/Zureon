# 🚀 Project Zureon

An automated tool designed to find local businesses, audit their online presence, and generate custom B2B marketing pitches with AI video prompts.

---

## 🛠️ How It Works

1. **Finds Businesses:** Searches for local target businesses (e.g., Car Dealers in Abuja).
2. **Audits Their Setup:** Checks if their website works, if they have SSL security, and if they respond quickly to customers.
3. **Creates the Pitch:** Generates a personalized compliment and an AI video storyboard to show them how to fix their gaps.
4. **Delivers the Alert:** Prepares outreach copy for WhatsApp/Email and sends an instant alert to your Telegram.
